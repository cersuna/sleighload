How to play Sleighload

Goal: fill every square of Santa's sleigh with the presents in the tray. No gaps, no overlaps.

Controls

  • Drag a present from the tray into the sleigh. A dashed outline shows where it will land.
  • Tap a present to rotate it 90°. This works in the tray or in the sleigh.
  • Drag a placed present to move it, or drag it off the sleigh to put it back in the tray.
  • 💡 Hint locks one present into its correct spot.
  • ↺ Unpack all sends every unlocked present back to the tray.

Difficulty by day

  • Monday & Tuesday: 5×5 sleigh (easy)
  • Wednesday to Friday: 6×6 (medium)
  • Saturday & Sunday: 7×7 (hard)

Scoring

Your time and moves are tracked. Solve with no hints for ⭐⭐⭐, 1–2 hints for ⭐⭐, more for ⭐. Solve on consecutive days to build a 🔥 streak.

Strategy tips

  1. Start with corners and edges. Corners can only be filled a few ways, so they narrow the options fast.
  2. Place the biggest, weirdest presents first. Small 3-square pieces are easy to fit into leftover gaps.
  3. Watch for dead zones. A 1- or 2-square hole can never be filled, because every present is at least 3 squares.
  4. Count squares. If a gap has 7 squares, it needs presents adding up to exactly 7.

FAQ

Is it free? Yes, free in any browser. No download, no account.
When is the new puzzle? Every day at midnight, your local time.
Is every puzzle solvable? Always. Each puzzle is made by cutting a full sleigh into presents.
Same puzzle for everyone? Yes, so you can race your friends.
