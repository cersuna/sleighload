What is Sleighload?

Sleighload is a free daily Christmas puzzle game. Every day there's one new sleigh and a pile of oddly shaped presents. Drag each present into the sleigh and tap to rotate it. When every square is filled, you win. Everyone in the world gets the same puzzle, so you can compare times with friends and family. Weekday puzzles start easy (5×5) and weekends get harder (7×7).
