Privacy policy

Sleighload has no accounts and no database. Your puzzle progress, times and streaks are saved only in your browser's local storage.

We may use Google Analytics to count visits and Google AdSense to show ads, which keeps the game free. These services may use cookies. You can manage ad personalization at Google Ad Settings.

Sponsored days are clearly labeled. Sponsors receive no data about players.
